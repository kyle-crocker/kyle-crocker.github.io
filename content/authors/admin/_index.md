Kyle is currently an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Physics and Biophysics at Augusta University](https://www.augusta.edu/scimath/physics/index.php).

Previously, he was an [Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Fellow](https://aiscience.uchicago.edu/) in the [Department of Ecology & Evolution](https://ecologyandevolution.uchicago.edu/) and [Center for Physics of Evolving Systems](https://news.uchicago.edu/story/eminent-bioengineering-scholar-lead-uchicagos-center-physics-evolving-systems) at the University of Chicago, advised by [Seppe Kuehn](https://www.kuehnlab.org/). Before beginning as a postdoc, he was a graduate student fellow at Ohio State, where he completed a PhD in physics with [Ralf Bundschuh](http://bioserv.mps.ohio-state.edu/~rbund/).
